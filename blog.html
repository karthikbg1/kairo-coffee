How to choose a specialty coffee subscription: the honest guide

There are a lot of coffee subscriptions out there these days. It can be hard to know which one is right for you. In this guide we will try to help you figure out what to look for.

What makes coffee "specialty"?

Specialty coffee is coffee that is really good quality. It comes from specific places and is treated carefully throughout the process. You can usually tell the difference when you drink it.

What to look for in a subscription

When choosing a subscription you should think about things like how fresh the coffee is, where it comes from, and whether the price feels right to you. Personal preference matters a lot too.

Red flags to avoid

Some subscriptions are not as good as they seem. Watch out for things that seem too good to be true or companies that don't tell you much about their coffee.
