Frequently asked questions

Questions about our coffee, subscriptions, and delivery.

How much does Kairo Coffee cost?
We have a few different plans at different price points to suit different budgets. Please see our shop page for current pricing.
Is Kairo Coffee good for beginners?
Yes, we think our coffee is suitable for most people including those who are new to specialty coffee. We try to make things easy to understand.
Where does Kairo Coffee source its beans?
We source from various countries around the world where great coffee is grown. We work with farmers who care about quality and sustainability.
How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?
We try to make sure the coffee is as fresh as possible when it reaches you. We roast regularly and ship promptly.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel at any time. Just get in touch with us and we will sort it out for you.
