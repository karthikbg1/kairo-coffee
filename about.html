About Kairo Coffee

Kairo Coffee was founded in London in 2021 by former consultant Karthik and specialists Vineesha and Omprava, who met while working for platform strategy in India.The brand sources exclusively from farms paying 40%+ above commodity price, with full chain-of-custody documentation for every single-origin coffee in the range.

Our sourcing

Kairo sources from three primary regions: the Yirgacheffe zone in Ethiopia (Konga Cooperative, Est. 1957), the Huila department in Colombia (Finca El Paraiso, altitude 1,850m), and Antigua, Guatemala (La Hermosa Estate). All farms are visited annually by our sourcing team and pay a minimum floor price of $2.80/lb — 38% above the 2026 commodity benchmark.
